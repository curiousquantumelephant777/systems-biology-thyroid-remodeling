- Anxiety felt worse
- Subjective symptoms consistent with thyrotoxicosis or hyperthyroidism

- Severe, unrelenting fatigue (suggesting dip into hypothyroid state)

  - Severe tingling/vibrating sensation in extremeties and lips, suggesting calcium dip or "hungry bone syndrome" (question: Is this due to parathyroid stunning or a potential overlooked intrathyroid parathyroid gland partially or fully hidden within the nodule?)

  - Difficulty processing thoughts (question: Could neural calcium and ionized calcium levels be a biomarker for mental illness and ADHD?)
